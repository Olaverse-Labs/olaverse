# Commercial Support

Olaverse is open source (Apache 2.0) and free to use in production. For teams that need more than the off-the-shelf models, we offer commercial engagements.

---

## Need custom language support?

The published models cover a specific set of languages and domains. If your product needs something different, we provide:

- ✅ **Fine-tuning** — adapt DiacNet, LID, rerankers, or embeddings to your domain and data
- ✅ **Custom datasets** — corpus building, cleaning, and annotation for underrepresented languages
- ✅ **Domain adaptation** — legal, medical, financial, or product-specific vocabulary
- ✅ **Deployment optimization** — quantization, CPU/edge targets, latency budgets
- ✅ **Enterprise integration** — embedding Olaverse components into your existing pipeline

---

## Why work with us

- **We built the models.** Every model in the SDK — DiacNet, the LID family, OTK-BPE, MIST, Prism — was trained by Olaverse Labs, so custom variants come from the people who know the training recipe.
- **Small-model philosophy.** We optimize for deployability: a 1.1 MB language detector and a 7 MB diacritizer run where a 7B model can't.
- **Honest benchmarks.** Published numbers are measured, limitations are documented on every model card — you'll know what you're getting before you commit.

## Contributing

Prefer to build in the open? We actively welcome contributions in:

- **Datasets** — parallel text, diacritized corpora, evaluation sets
- **Language support** — new languages for LID, DiacNet, and tokenizers
- **Evaluation** — benchmark runs, error analysis, per-language reports
- **Documentation** — examples, tutorials, translations

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/Olaverse-Labs/olaverse/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md) to get started.
